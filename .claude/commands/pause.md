---
description: Stop the job search running
---

Create `~/Meester/PAUSED` (an empty file, no extension). Confirm it exists.

Then tell her, clearly, both halves:

- Everything is stopped: no harvesting, no applications, no mail reading.
- **Updates are stopped too.** If she pushes a fix now, it will not install on its
  own. The **Update available — install** button in the app still works while
  paused, or `/unpause` first.

Mention she can also do this in Finder without a terminal, and that `/unpause`
undoes it.
